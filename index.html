The philosophy

Why this works

Build the raw materials. Collagen and elastin are made from glycine, proline, vitamin C, retinol, zinc and copper — all abundant in an animal-based plate of meat, fish, eggs, liver, shellfish and bone broth. High protein also protects lean mass while you lose fat.

Stop the two things that age skin. Advanced glycation end-products (AGEs) stiffen collagen — driven by excess sugar (especially fructose) and by charred, high-heat cooking. Seed-oil PUFAs oxidize into inflammatory aldehydes. Cut both, and cook gentle and moist.

Lose fat, gently. A modest 10–20% calorie deficit with high protein sheds fat while sparing the collagen and elasticity that crash diets burn through.

Please read
